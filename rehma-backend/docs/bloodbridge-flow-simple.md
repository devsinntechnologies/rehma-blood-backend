# Rehma BloodBridge — Simple Flow Guide

**For everyone using the app (not for developers)**

---

## Who does what?

| Role | Who they are | Main job |
|------|----------------|----------|
| **Requester** | Patient, family, or hospital staff | Ask for blood and confirm it was received |
| **Donor** | Person who can give blood | Respond to requests and complete the donation |
| **Admin** | Rehma support team | Help when there is a disagreement or old records need fixing |

---

## Step-by-step: from request to “thank you”

### 1. Someone needs blood
The requester opens the app and creates a **blood request**. They add:
- Blood group and **how many units** are needed  
- **Hospital** and **location**  
- **Contact phone** and **deadline** (how urgent it is)  
- Any notes for the donor  

They can **track** the request at any time and see how many units are still needed.

---

### 2. The system finds donors
BloodBridge **invites matching donors** (a few at a time, so people are not spammed).  
If a donor does not answer in time, the system can **invite others** automatically.

Matching **stops** when the request is filled, cancelled, expired, or paused.

---

### 3. The donor gets notified
Donors receive a **notification** (and can open the request from the app).  
They see hospital, location, blood group, units needed, deadline, and contact details.

**The donor chooses one of:**
- **Accept** — “I will help.” If more than one unit is needed, they say **how many units** they commit to.  
- **Decline** — “I cannot help this time.”  
- **Available later** — “Not now, but maybe later.”  

If someone manages a donor on their behalf, the app shows that clearly.

---

### 4. After accepting — “My Current Commitment”
On the home screen, an accepting donor sees **My Current Commitment**:  
request details, units they promised, and what to do next (e.g. go to hospital, report when done).

---

### 5. Donation day
The donor goes to the hospital and donates. In the app they **report donation** and say **how many units** they actually gave.

- If they promised 2 but only report 1, the app keeps the rest as **still committed** until they update or release it.  
- The requester is notified that donation was reported.

---

### 6. Requester confirms receipt
The requester checks what arrived at the hospital and **confirms receipt** (how many units they actually received).

- They can confirm in **parts** (e.g. 1 now, 1 later) as long as it does not exceed what was reported.  
- When enough units are confirmed, the request moves toward **complete**.

---

### 7. If something does not match — dispute
If the requester believes the numbers or facts are wrong, they can **open a dispute**.  
Status is held until **Admin** reviews and decides (confirm the donation or reject the report), with a reason on record.

---

### 8. Cancellation
The requester can **cancel** an open request (with a reason).  
Donors who had accepted may **withdraw** if their plans change.  
Cancelled or expired requests no longer invite new donors.

---

### 9. Admin (when needed)
Admins can:
- See **tracking** for a request (units required, reserved, reported, confirmed, remaining)  
- **Resolve disputes**  
- **Reconcile** older requests that existed before BloodBridge (without guessing wrong numbers)

---

## Units — in plain language

Think of **units** as bags of blood.

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Required** | How many the patient still needs |
| **Reserved** | Promised by donors but not yet reported donated |
| **Reported** | Donor says they gave this many; waiting for hospital confirmation |
| **Confirmed** | Requester says the hospital received this many |
| **Remaining** | Still needed after confirmed amounts |

Several donors can help **one request** when multiple units are needed.

---

## Notifications (short)

- Donors and requesters get alerts for invites, acceptances, reports, confirmations, disputes, and cancellations.  
- Tapping a notification opens the **right request** in the app (after sign-in if needed).

---

## One-page summary

1. **Request** → hospital, location, blood group, units, deadline, phone  
2. **Invite donors** → accept / decline / available later  
3. **Donor commits** → shown on home as current commitment  
4. **Report donation** → how many units donated  
5. **Confirm receipt** → how many units received  
6. **Dispute or admin** → only if something is wrong  
7. **Done** when enough units are confirmed or request is cancelled  

*Rehma BloodBridge — connecting requesters and donors safely and clearly.*
